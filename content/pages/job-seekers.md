## Join the Bluestar Care Team
- **Tagline**: Collectively, we will work hand in hand to turn this vision into a reality!
- **Core Message**:
  > If you have prior professional experience in a care home, hospital, or home care setting, that’s fantastic! We value your expertise and would be delighted to consider you for available positions.
  >
  > Alternatively, if you haven’t worked in the care field before but have cared for a family member or friend, we have a limited number of roles available for individuals with caregiving experience outside of a professional setting. We are committed to providing the necessary training and support to help you transition into your new career.

## What We Look For
- Sincere compassion and empathy for others
- Effective verbal and written communication skills
- Ability to comprehend and follow care plans and instructions
- Reliable transportation and flexible availability (advantageous)

- **Competitive Pay Rates** (Real Living Wage Employer)
- **Flexible Scheduling** (part-time, full-time, weekend shifts, live-in shifts)
- **Extensive Training & Mentorship** (Care Certificate, mandatory training, specialist certifications)
- **Ongoing Support** from experienced care managers
- **Modern Candidate Mobile App** for fast shift booking and digital timesheets

## Interactive Contact Form Specification
- **Form Action**: Submits inquiry / candidate CV to administration
- **Fields**:
  1. `First Name` (text, required)
  2. `Last Name` (text, required)
  3. `Email Address` (email, required)
  4. `Phone Number` (tel, required)
  5. `Inquiry Type` (select: General Inquiry / Care Services for Family / Job Application / Live-in Care)
  6. `Message` (textarea, required)
  7. `Upload CV / Document` (file upload: PDF, DOC, DOCX - optional/required for job seekers)
  8. `Consent Checkbox` ("I agree to the privacy policy and consent to Bluestar Care contacting me regarding my inquiry")
  9. `Submit Button`: "Send Message"

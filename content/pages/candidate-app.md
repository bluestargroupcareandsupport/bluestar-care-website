## App Overview
> The mobile app is powered by an advanced staffing solution (Logezy), designed to help candidates stay engaged with the vacancies available to us. As a registered candidate, you will have powerful tools at your disposal enabling you to stay updated on vacancies, update availability instantly, view bookings, and sign timesheets on the app itself. The Candidate App makes it convenient for you as a candidate to make sure that you never miss a shift.

---

## App Features Breakdown
1. **Instantly Apply For Vacancies** (`Pegasus-job-location.png`)
   - Candidates can apply for shifts with a single click. Notifications arrive as soon as vacancies are published.
2. **Shift Allocation & Booking** (`Pegasus-book.png`, `Pegasus-allocation.png`)
   - View confirmed upcoming shifts, client locations, and shift requirements.
3. **Availability Management** (`Pegasus-availability.png`)
   - Set available days, preferred times, and blackout dates in real-time.
4. **Digital Timesheets & Three-way Verification** (`Pegasus-timesheet.png`, `Pegasus-threeway.png`)
   - Clock in/out, record hours, and get supervisor digital sign-offs right on your smartphone.
5. **Compliance Tracking & Expiry Alerts** (`Pegasus-compliance.png`, `Pegasus-expired.png`)
   - Track DBS certificates, training renewals, right to work documents, and avoid shift downtime.
6. **Holiday & Leave Requests** (`Pegasus-holiday.png`)
   - Submit annual leave requests directly to the coordination team.

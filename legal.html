Legal Notices & Terms

1. Operating Entities & Data Protection

This website is operated by Bluestar Care & Support Ltd. Registered office: 2nd Floor, 40a The Boulevard, Crawley, West Sussex, RH10 1XP.

For data protection, privacy, and statutory compliance enquiries, please contact our administrative desk at jobs@bluestar-caresupport.co.uk or by telephone on 01293 300009.

2. Privacy Policy — Client & Candidate Data

Contact details and healthcare assessment records are maintained strictly in compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and Data Protection Act 2018. Personal information relating to an end-user or candidate is shared with staff only to the extent necessary to deliver safe care or arrange staffing shifts.

Digital records are password-protected and encrypted. You have the right to request details of your personal data, request corrections, or object to data processing at any time by contacting our registered office.

3. Copyright Notice

The copyright in all original material, layout, and images on this website is owned by Bluestar Care & Support Ltd. Material may be downloaded for personal informational use only but may not be reproduced or altered without prior written permission.

4. Disclaimer

This website is intended to be informative. Bluestar Care & Support Ltd takes reasonable precautions to ensure content is accurate and up to date, but does not warrant its application to individual clinical circumstances without a formal professional care plan. Nothing within this website constitutes a binding healthcare contract until a formal care agreement is signed.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential operational cookies to ensure the website works securely and effectively. You can adjust your browser settings to decline or remove cookies at any time.

Who We Are

First Step Toward Better Living

We are a dedicated group of caring professionals at Bluestar Care & Support Ltd who are deeply committed to improving the lives of those we serve.

Founded on foundational principles of empathy, excellence, and integrity, our mission is to offer personalized support that caters to the unique needs, dignity, and desires of each individual client.

We work in collaboration with families, GPs, district nurses, and social workers to deliver seamless care that enables individuals to continue enjoying their homes and communities safely.

Our Philosophy

We believe in providing care that goes far beyond basic daily needs. We focus on comprehensive, holistic well-being that includes physical, emotional, social, and mental health.

Our holistic approach ensures that our clients not only receive the essential assistance they need, but also enjoy enriched, connected, and truly fulfilling lives.

Our Vision

Bluestar Care & Support Ltd envisions a world where care and support are not merely duties, but heartfelt connections that empower individuals to live with dignity, grace, and joy.

We strive to create an environment where every client feels at home, genuinely valued, and part of our extended family.

Our People

Our Dedicated Care Team

Our dedicated team of caregivers, support staff, and healthcare professionals are at the very heart of our success.

Highly trained and committed to our core values, they work collaboratively to deliver quality care with warmth and respect. Their unwavering commitment ensures that Bluestar is a name synonymous with trust and excellence in care.

Rigorous Standards & Governance

Every member of our team undergoes thorough enhanced DBS background checks, in-depth reference verifications, and ongoing mandatory training covering safeguarding, medication administration, moving and handling, and infection control.

Accreditations & Badges

Our Commitment To Ethical Healthcare

We hold ourselves to the highest standards of governance, fair employment, and regulatory compliance.

Real Living Wage Employer

We believe in paying fair, accredited real living wages to all healthcare workers, ensuring high staff retention, motivation, and continuity of care for our clients.

REC Corporate Member

As members of the Recruitment & Employment Confederation, we adhere to the highest codes of professional recruitment practice and ethics in healthcare staffing.

CQC Aligned Protocols

We operate under strict clinical governance standards, aligned with Care Quality Commission requirements for safety, effectiveness, responsiveness, and leadership.

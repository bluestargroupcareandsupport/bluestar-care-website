Candidate Mobile Staffing App

Home › Candidate App
Powered by Logezy Staffing Solutions

Find Healthcare Shifts At Your Fingertips

The mobile app is powered by an advanced staffing solution, designed to help candidates stay engaged with vacancies available across Bluestar Care & Support Ltd.

As a registered candidate, you will have powerful tools at your disposal enabling you to stay updated on vacancies, update availability instantly, view bookings, and sign digital timesheets directly on the app itself.

The Candidate App makes it convenient for you as a healthcare professional to ensure that you never miss a shift and get paid promptly with electronic verification.

App Capabilities

Powerful Tools For Modern Care Workers

Everything you need to manage your healthcare shifts, availability, and earnings right on your smartphone.

Instant Shift Alerts

Receive push notifications the second new shifts and vacancies are published. Apply with a single tap before shifts are filled.

Live Shift Calendar

Access your complete upcoming schedule, shift allocations, client addresses, and specific care plan guidelines 24/7.

Availability Toggle

Set your working hours in seconds. Mark days available, update preferences, or set blackout dates instantly without calling the office.

Digital Timesheets

Clock in and out, record completed shift hours, and capture digital signatures from clients or managers right on your mobile screen.

Compliance Monitor

Track the status of your DBS check, training certificates, and right-to-work documents. Receive advance notifications before any certification expires.

Leave & Holiday Requests

Submit annual leave or time-off requests directly through the app and receive instant confirmations from coordination managers.

Join Our Healthcare Team

Collectively, We Will Work Hand In Hand To Turn This Vision Into Reality!

If you have prior professional experience in a care home, hospital, or domiciliary care setting, that’s fantastic! We value your expertise and would be delighted to consider you for available positions across West Sussex and surrounding regions.

Alternatively, if you haven’t worked in the professional care field before but have cared for a family member or friend, we have roles available for individuals with informal caregiving background. We provide full, accredited training and mentorship to help you transition into a fulfilling, long-term healthcare career.

Apply Online Now Email Your CV

What We Look For

  • Sincere Compassion & Empathy: A genuine desire to make a meaningful difference in the lives of vulnerable adults and seniors.
  • Effective Communication Skills: Active listening, clear verbal communication, and accurate care-log record keeping.
  • Reliability & Integrity: Punctuality, respect for client confidentiality, and adherence to individualized care plans.
  • Transport & Availability: Access to a vehicle and driver's license is advantageous, along with flexible daytime, evening, or weekend availability.

What We Offer

  • Real Living Wage Rates: Accredited fair pay with enhanced weekend and holiday rates.
  • Flexible Shift Patterns: Choose shift schedules that align with your lifestyle, family, or studies.
  • Paid Training & Qualifications: Full induction, Care Certificate, moving & handling, dementia training, and NVQ sponsorship.
  • Dedicated Staff Support: Instant notifications for available shifts, digital timesheets, and fast weekly pay processing.
